## The default rates

- **EPCOR Energy Alberta** (Rate of Last Resort (default)): 12.01¢/kWh + $0.230/day per site admin. Fixed to Dec 31, 2026.
- **ENMAX Energy** (Rate of Last Resort (default)): 12.06¢/kWh + $0.4134/day per site (2026 interim tariff) admin. Fixed to Dec 31, 2026.
- **Direct Energy Regulated Services** (Default Rate Tariff (October 2026)): $1.458/GJ + $0.385/day per site admin. Changes monthly.
- Default gas averaged $2.025/GJ over November 2025 to October 2026; it resets monthly and usually rises in winter.

**73 days until the Rate of Last Resort resets (Jan 1, 2027).** The new two-year rate must be filed at least 30 days before, and an adjustment normally can't exceed 10%. Leaving the RoLR has no penalty. [What it means](https://albertaadraws.github.io/alberta-power/rate-of-last-resort-2027/)

## Water and solar

- **Water is not a competitive market.** Edmonton's first water tier is $2.5258/m³ (EPCOR, plus daily fixed charges) and Calgary's water rate is $1.7409/m³. You cannot switch providers. [Edmonton water rates](https://albertaadraws.github.io/alberta-power/water/)
- **Alberta solar club rates:** published HI export rate 35.00¢/kWh, LO import 5.90¢/kWh. [Full comparison](https://albertaadraws.github.io/alberta-power/solar/rates/) (export rate, import rate, admin fee, source).
- Programs, in the words of their own pages: City of Edmonton Clean Energy Improvement Program (CEIP) (Waitlist (residential pre-qualification)); City of Calgary Clean Energy Improvement Program (residential) (Intake closed until winter 2026/2027); Canada Greener Homes Loan (Ended (funding fully committed)); Canada Greener Homes Grant (Ended (last documents December 31, 2025)); Alberta provincial solar programs (No provincial solar program (UCA)). [Solar in Alberta](https://albertaadraws.github.io/alberta-power/solar/)
- NRCan annual PV potential, south-facing at latitude tilt: Edmonton 1244 kWh/kW, Calgary 1291 kWh/kW. [Payback estimate](https://albertaadraws.github.io/alberta-power/solar/#payback)
